Project description

A small agency sends a 2,000-USDC invoice and receives 1,200. Contractors still need to be paid, but spending the whole receipt would leave the project without working cash. Splitt helps the owner decide what can be paid today, what can wait under agreed terms, and what must stay reserved.

The owner records approved contractor commitments, payment terms and project limits. Splitt proposes an allocation with reasons linked to structured facts. A deterministic validator checks eligibility, cumulative obligations, partial-payment rules, reserves, daily limits and stale state. Optional model planning is labelled separately from the deterministic baseline. The owner reviews the exact recipients and amounts before authorizing a batch.

The ProjectVault contract enforces project isolation, single-use execution IDs, atomic payouts and an EIP-712 signature bound to the chain, vault, project version, deadline and ordered payment items. Arc Testnet is the target settlement network. Circle developer-controlled wallet execution, receipt indexing and a PostgreSQL-backed queue are implemented, with recovery tests. A missing provider response keeps the batch unresolved until replay flags can establish its outcome.

The local demo demonstrates a 700-USDC full obligation, a permitted 200-USDC partial payment and a protected 300-USDC reserve. When the 800 remainder arrives, the new plan pays only the outstanding 300. These figures are a fixture scenario, not customer activity. A scaled 2-USDC scenario ran on the deployed Arc vault on 15 October: 0.7 and 0.2 USDC paid, 0.8 received, then the final 0.3 paid. These were controlled test wallets. No pilot business is verified.

Source verification is independently confirmed by Sourcify with exact creation and runtime bytecode matches. The payout-and-remainder flow and lost-response recovery are proven. Receipt-triggered drafts and payment-note evidence matching are implemented. CCTP invoice funding is implemented and tested, but no completed cross-chain transfer is proven. Yield management and production customer payments are not claimed.
